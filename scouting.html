How to read the numbers

The six rating bars carry forward the original editorial scores on a 0–100 scale. Confidence describes the available evidence for each rating. The sample filter changes measured habits and game results. Editorial ratings and featured stories use the broader profile.

A move attempt is an action logged by the replay, including failed attempts. A guard can set up without blocking a hit. “Blocked” counts an attempt at most once even if both slots were protected. Trick Room activations count verified starts. Reversal and expiry are separate effects.

Opening-of-turn switches exclude drag events, replacements after a logged move and move-driven pivots. They approximate voluntary switches. “Brought” means observed entrants. A back-slot Pokémon that stayed off the field cannot be identified.

Roster-based rates use the original draft and transaction records. Playoff and Monotype views show replay preview and entry data. Types follow the revealed form when available, and a dual type counts under both types.
